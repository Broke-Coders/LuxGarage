Description

The McLaren 650S Spider delivers an incredible open-top driving experience paired with unmistakable British design. Powered by a twin-turbocharged 3.8L V8 engine, it produces exhilarating performance with a lightning-fast dual-clutch transmission and rear-wheel drive. The power-operated soft top opens in seconds, allowing you to enjoy the sound of the V8 at any speed. Finished with a sharp, aggressive exterior and a driver-focused interior, the 650S Spider offers the perfect balance of supercar performance, luxury, and everyday usability.
